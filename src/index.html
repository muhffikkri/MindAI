Box Breathing

Breathing

A simple technique to calm your nervous system by controlling your breath in a 4-4-4-4 pattern.

  • Inhale for 4 seconds
  • Hold for 4 seconds
  • Exhale for 4 seconds
  • Hold for 4 seconds
Learn more →

5-4-3-2-1 Grounding

Grounding

Use your senses to ground yourself in the present moment and reduce anxiety.

  • 5 things you can see
  • 4 things you can touch
  • 3 things you can hear
  • 2 things you can smell
  • 1 thing you can taste
Learn more →

Progressive Relaxation

Relaxation

Systematically tense and release different muscle groups to release physical tension.

  • Start from your toes
  • Tense muscles for 5 seconds
  • Release and notice the feeling
  • Move up through your body
Learn more →

Mindfulness Meditation

Meditation

Practice observing your thoughts without judgment to develop mental clarity and emotional balance.

  • Find a comfortable position
  • Focus on your breath
  • Notice thoughts passing
  • Return focus gently
